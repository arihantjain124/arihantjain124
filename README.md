I’m a research engineer at IIT Madras with an M.S. in Electrical Engineering. My work sits at the intersection of deep learning, medical-image reconstruction, and agentic AI. I build and evaluate practical models in PyTorch, with a particular interest in 3D vision and GPU-aware optimisation.

## Selected work

### [FastDTI — 3D scale-arbitrary super-resolution](https://github.com/arihantjain124/HCP_SR)

A single-pass 3D model that reconstructs clinical diffusion-tensor maps from seven input volumes. **Published at SASHIMI, MICCAI 2025**; achieved the best PSNR/NMSE among evaluated methods with ~6× faster inference than diffusion-model baselines.

`PyTorch` `3D CNNs` `Medical imaging`

### [Electoral Bond Analysis](https://github.com/arihantjain124/ElectoralBond)

Reproducible analysis and visualisation of India’s public electoral-bond disclosures, from raw-data cleaning to publication-ready charts.

`Python` `Pandas` `Plotly`

### [Sudoku Capture Solver](https://github.com/arihantjain124/Sudoku-Capture-Solver)

A camera-to-solution computer-vision pipeline that detects a printed Sudoku, reads its cells with a CNN, solves the grid, and overlays the result.

`OpenCV` `TensorFlow` `Keras`

### [Neural networks in CUDA](https://github.com/arihantjain124/CS6023_CP)

From-scratch CUDA/C++ experiments in GPU matrix operations and the precision–memory trade-offs behind neural-network training.

`CUDA` `C++`
